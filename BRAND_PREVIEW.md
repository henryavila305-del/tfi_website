# TFI stadium brand preview

Review only. Production main and thefavindex.com remain unchanged.

Replaces the abstract hero with a stadium composition, metallic type treatment, electric-lime display headlines, real Broncos/Giants/Bruins team marks and the completed November 8 public card. The completed card is explicitly historical. Preserves all four cumulative record tables and chart series byte-for-byte, record switching, mobile navigation, FAQ controls, anchors and the two official Whop links. All Access remains the primary membership.

Brand marks currently use a CSS metallic TFI wordmark and crown; this is a preview treatment, not an assertion that the original logo asset has been restored. Team marks are embedded from ESPN CDN (Denver Broncos, New York Giants, Boston Bruins). Anton display font is embedded under the SIL Open Font License, copyright Vernon Adams. Stadium art is CSS. No players or people. No live feed or private Premium selections are added.
