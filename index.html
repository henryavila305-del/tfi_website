05 / BEHIND THE INDEX

A process you
can question.

The Favorite Index is a home for sports perspective. The goal of this public site is simple: make published selections and their outcomes easier to understand.

01

Context before conviction

Public pick notes should explain the market, price, and reasoning at the time of publication. Odds can change.

02

A record with receipts

Results will need a dated selection, recorded odds, stake units, and a grading source. Pending and void outcomes should be identified separately.

03

Discipline over certainty

No selection is a guarantee. Past results do not predict future outcomes. Only risk what you can afford to lose.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the next play.

Are these live picks and results?

No. This Phase 1 preview has no connected picks or results feed. Empty states remain until approved public information is available.

How do I join Premium?

The verified official Whop membership link will be added before launch. Review pricing and membership terms on Whop. No private picks are included here.

How will performance be measured?

The proposed record will distinguish wins, losses, pushes, voids, and pending selections. Net units and ROI require verified odds and stakes. Detailed grading rules must be approved before results are published.
