How to read this record

Win rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses). Pushes and voids do not count as decisions. Opening balances are included; they represent earlier cumulative results rather than individual dated picks. Lines connect recorded dates and do not imply picks on missing days.

Premium overlaps Top 3 on ten entries. Each belongs to both category records but is one wager; category totals should not be added together. Bonus parlays and the separate NFL summary are excluded from these four graphs.

Source: the owner’s tracker workbook. Results are self-reported historical imports, not independently verified bets or proof of publication before an event. Individual Premium selections remain private.

Coverage excludes July 13–18, August 5–11 and September 1–6. September 26 and October 27 are not entered. Play of the Day has no recovered opening history. Eighteen prices are unavailable during vacation days; no replacement prices are assumed. Net units and ROI are not presented because the pricing history is incomplete.

05 / BEHIND THE INDEX

THE WORK
BEHIND THE PICKS.

The Favorite Index brings the picks and the record into one place. Clear selections. Results you can revisit. A process that stays accountable.

01

Context before conviction

Public pick notes should explain the market, price, and reasoning at the time of publication. Odds can change.

02

A record with receipts

Results will need a dated selection, recorded odds, stake units, and a grading source. Pending and void outcomes should be identified separately.

03

Discipline over certainty

No selection is a guarantee. Past results do not predict future outcomes. Only risk what you can afford to lose.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

KNOW THE PLAY.

Are these live picks and results?

The results section shows imported historical records, with category coverage dates. There is no live picks or results feed connected. The free picks board remains empty until selections are published.

How do I join Premium?

Join TFI Premium through our official Whop membership page using the button above. Review pricing and membership terms on Whop. No private picks are included here.

How will performance be measured?

Historical win rate uses wins divided by wins plus losses. Pushes and voids are excluded. Imported opening balances are included and missing dates are disclosed beside the graphs. Net units and ROI are withheld because the odds history is incomplete.
